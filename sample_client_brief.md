# Architectural Client Brief & Requirement Specification

## 1. Project Overview & Site Parameters
* **Project Name**: Villa Harmony (ECR Residence)
* **Location**: East Coast Road (ECR), Chennai, Tamil Nadu
* **Site Context**: Coastal environment, humid tropical climate zone
* **Plot Dimensions**: 40 ft (Width) × 60 ft (Depth) = **2,400 sq ft Total Plot Area**
* **Site Orientation**: East-facing road (y = 0 ft road edge)
* **Bylaw Regulation**: TNCDBR 2019 (Non-High Rise Building, Max Height 39 ft / G+2 floors)
* **Required Setbacks**: Front: 5 ft | Rear: 5 ft | Side: 5 ft
* **Target Budget**: ₹85,000,000 (₹85 Lakhs inclusive of plinth structure, finishes, MEP, & car porch)

## 2. Household Profile & Accessibility Needs
* **Occupancy**: Family of 5 (2 Adults, 2 Children, 1 Elderly Resident)
* **Special Considerations**: 
  * Ground floor bedroom & step-free ensuite bathroom required for elderly resident access.
  * Passive solar design with East-facing kitchen/morning light and West sun screening.
  * Cross-ventilation corridors for coastal humidity mitigation.

## 3. Ground Floor Architectural Program (G.F. Built-up Area: ~1,250 sq ft)
1. **Car Porch / Parking Bay**: 15 ft × 14 ft (Covered single-car parking at North-East entrance)
2. **Foyer & Main Living Room**: 18 ft × 14 ft (Double-height volume with cross ventilation facing East)
3. **Dining Room**: 14 ft × 12 ft (Central circulation hub connecting Living & Kitchen)
4. **Kitchen**: 11 ft × 12 ft (South-East Agni quadrant layout with granite counter)
5. **Utility / Wash Area**: 5 ft × 12 ft (Attached to kitchen for laundry & dry wash)
6. **Ground Floor Master Bedroom (Elderly)**: 14 ft × 14 ft (South-West quadrant for quiet privacy)
7. **Attached Bathroom 1**: 8 ft × 8 ft (Step-free shower, anti-skid floor tiles)
8. **Staircase Core**: 5 ft × 12 ft (Dog-legged staircase located along central load-bearing axis)

## 4. First Floor Architectural Program (F.F. Built-up Area: ~1,150 sq ft)
1. **First Floor Master Bedroom**: 14 ft × 14 ft (Structurally stacked over ground floor master bedroom)
2. **Ensuite Bathroom 2**: 8 ft × 8 ft (Premium vitrified tiles, glass shower enclosure)
3. **Children's Bedroom 3**: 14 ft × 14 ft (North-West quadrant with ample study daylight)
4. **Bathroom 3 (Attached/Common)**: 8 ft × 7 ft
5. **Home Office / Study**: 10 ft × 8 ft (Quiet workspace with high-speed cabling setup)
6. **Covered Sit-out Balcony**: 14 ft × 8 ft (Overlooking front garden with glass & MS railing)

## 5. Structural & Architectural Specifications
* **Foundation**: Reinforced Concrete Isolated Footings (SBC 150 kN/m²)
* **Superstructure**: RCC Framed Structure with M25 Concrete & Fe500 Steel Reinforcement
* **Exterior Masonry**: 9-inch wire-cut clay bricks with Weather-shield acrylic paint
* **Roofing & Exterior**: Sloping terracotta Mangalore tile roof with solar panel mounting, rain-water harvesting sump, and traditional Thinnai sit-out at front.
